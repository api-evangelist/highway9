# Highway 9 Networks

> Highway 9 Networks delivers AI Wireless Infrastructure as a Service through its Virtual Mobile/AI Cloud (VMC) platform — combining a cloud control plane (Mobile Center), a distributed 5G core and edge AI services (Mobile Edge), private 5G wireless (Mobile Network), and connected device/workload groupings (Mobile Zones) to give enterprises deterministic, ultra-reliable private mobile networks and in-building cellular coverage.

Highway 9 serves manufacturing and warehousing (autonomous robots, drones, logistics), higher education (AI labs and research), and energy (predictive maintenance, remote operations). The Mobile Center Streaming APIs let signed partners integrate configuration, device management, monitoring, analytics, and operational workflows with the platform. The full OpenAPI reference is available inside the partner portal for signed partners and is not published publicly.

## APIs

- [Mobile Center Streaming APIs](https://www.highway9.com/mobile-center-streaming-apis/): Partner integration API for configuration, device management (DeviceInfo, DeviceGroup), alerts (Alarm, alertProfile, alertsTemplate), telemetry and audit logs (AuditLog), security (apiToken, Certificate), application management (Application, Attribute), operator/carrier metadata (carrierInfo, mnoOperator), and assistive services (chatbot). Base URL: https://partner.demo.highway9networks.com/api/v1/orch
